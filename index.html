// Good To Know

Frequently asked questions.

How do I place an order? +

Message us on WhatsApp or call 976-8756185 with what you'd like and your delivery address. We'll confirm the price and estimated time before dispatching.

Do you check ID on delivery? +

Yes. Our riders may ask for identification to confirm you're of legal drinking age in Nepal before handing over your order.

What areas do you deliver to? +

We currently cover central Kathmandu, including the 44600 area and nearby neighborhoods. Message us your location and we'll confirm if we can reach you.

How do I pay? +

Cash on delivery or digital wallet transfer — whichever is easier for you. We'll confirm the total before your order leaves.

Are you really open 24 hours? +

Yes, every day of the week. Late-night and early-morning orders are some of the most common ones we get.
